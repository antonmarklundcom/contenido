# Paraguay B-Roll Video Ideas — Brief for AI Video Prompting

## Task

Generate AI video b-roll clips (9:16 vertical, ~6-8s each) for social media content
and site backgrounds, covering real estate, land, investment, and residency topics
in Paraguay. Output will be used as: (1) standalone social clips (Reels/TikTok/
Shorts) to talk over or caption, (2) cutaway b-roll behind narration, (3) candidate
footage for website hero sections once sites are finished.

## Businesses / sites this serves

**Real estate & land cluster** (Spanish-market, local buyers/investors):
- `inmobiliaria.com.py` — real estate listings/agency
- `realestateinparaguay.com` — real estate for foreign buyers
- `terreno.com.py` — land/plots specifically

**Residency & investor cluster** (English-market, foreigners considering Paraguay):
- `paraguayresidency.co.uk`
- `paraguayresidencyguide.com`
- `paraguayinvestorpass.com`

Note: some of these sites are not 100% finished — footage is being generated now for
social content first, and will be reused for site hero sections later.

## What we already tried and learned (read before generating)

Tested with Higgsfield Seedance 2.5, 9:16, 6s, 720p. Quality was average overall.
Two specific ideas that did NOT work well and should be avoided or reworked:

- **A single person walking slowly along a riverside path** (Costanera de Asunción,
  dusk) — came out weak/generic, not worth the credits as a "lone figure walking"
  concept.
- **A close-up macro zoom on a table object** (a terere thermos/cup on a café table,
  camera pushing in) — object-focused macro shots did not render convincingly.

**Takeaway for new prompts:** favor wider shots with more going on in the frame —
motion, multiple elements, environmental detail, aerial/drone movement — over slow,
minimal, single-subject compositions. Crowds, traffic, architecture, and landscape
scale seem to give the model more to work with than an isolated person or object.

## What makes a shot read as authentically Paraguayan

Use these as concrete, specific details in every prompt — generic "Latin America"
footage will not read as Paraguay specifically:

- **Landmarks**: Bahía de Asunción, Río Paraguay, Palacio de los López, Costanera de
  Asunción, Panteón de los Héroes, Mercado 4, Mercado Municipal
- **Nature**: lapacho trees (pink and yellow bloom), tierra colorada (red clay soil),
  palm trees, wide open cattle-ranch landscapes, the Chaco (dry scrubland, west) vs.
  Oriental region (green, humid, east)
- **Culture**: terere (iced yerba mate) with guampa and bombilla, chipa (cheese
  bread), ñandutí lace, colectivo buses (often older, colorful), motorbikes as
  primary transport, Guaraní language mixed into signage/speech, asado (grilled
  meat) culture
- **Urban texture**: mix of colonial-era buildings and modern glass towers, informal
  street vendors, red-brick and stucco low-rise neighborhoods, construction cranes
  signaling active development
- **Light**: strong subtropical sun, golden-hour warmth, humid haze in wide shots

## Prompt-writing rules for whichever AI generates these

1. Every prompt should be 3-5 sentences: subject + environment + camera movement +
   lighting/mood + ambient sound (no music unless specified).
2. Specify aspect ratio 9:16, duration 6-8s, resolution 720p (budget tier) unless
   testing a hero-candidate at higher res.
3. Prefer camera movement (drone push, dolly, tracking) over static/locked shots —
   motion reads better than stillness on this model.
4. Avoid: single isolated figures walking slowly, extreme macro/product close-ups,
   anything requiring precise hand/finger detail.
5. Always name at least one Paraguay-specific visual marker (see list above) per
   prompt — don't let it default to generic stock-footage Latin America.

## Notes for whichever AI you hand this to

- Keep the "avoid" list above in mind for every generated prompt — no lone walkers,
  no macro object zooms.
- If the target model supports it, request `generate_audio: true` for ambient sound
  (traffic, wind, chatter) rather than adding music — music was not part of what
  worked/didn't work yet, so keep testing without it first.
- Real estate cluster ideas (1–18) can be reused/cropped across all three Spanish
  real-estate domains. Residency cluster ideas (19–36) across all three English
  residency/investor domains. General culture (37–50) can support either cluster or
  stand alone as brand b-roll for contenido.com.py itself.
